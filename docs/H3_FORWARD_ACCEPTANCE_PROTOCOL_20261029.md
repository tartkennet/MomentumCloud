## Fixed period and economic criteria

Under the existing frozen accounting, the first complete interval starts at the first observed November 2026 close and ends at the first observed December close. Trading-session counts are reported separately; no invented fixed number of daily observations replaces the monthly requirement.

At maturity both existing 15 and 30bp accounts must satisfy the unchanged five gates: at least 30 periods, positive net total return, positive net total edge over separately costed SPY, daily max drawdown no worse than −20%, and positive-period hit rate at least 50%. Sharpe is descriptive, not an additional tuned pass threshold. Currency USD, gross of tax, zero cash interest and zero risk-free rate, sample daily standard deviation and sqrt(252). ## Integrity and execution

The source archive must match the frozen commit. Each run verifies the prior record, source identity and the unchanged equity/trade/target prefix in all four accounts. No more than one previously unrecorded market session may be processed. New intentions must be recorded before 12:00 UTC. Missing originals, gaps, invalid input or changed past rows block the journal; no intermediate decisions are reconstructed retrospectively. Reruns produce distinct immutable run/attempt archives; the mutable latest pointer advances only after its immutable snapshot and inactive encrypted cache slot are uploaded. Raw licensed inputs remain authenticated and encrypted.

Fills remain the frozen model's actual next-session raw open with whole-share, available-cash and fee rules. They are simulated fills, not actual broker executions. Fees at 15/30bp include a declared cost allowance and are computed on actual simulated notional; actual brokerage commissions are not claimed. Record volume participation from observed raw daily volume, and NBBO spread/quoted-size observations when the EXISTING subscription permits them. A quote proxy cannot certify an opening-auction fill, queue position, market impact or depth. Missing quote access or incomplete execution evidence remains visible and prevents live readiness; no new subscription is purchased and no frozen NAV/fill is rewritten using later quotes.

The current subscription probe returned HTTP 403 for historical quotes. Consequently the deployed observer reports spread and opening depth as UNVERIFIED, and actual brokerage fees as NOT_OBSERVED. There is no claim of a working per-fill quote collector. A later independent execution review must collect sufficient contemporaneous evidence before live readiness can be considered; upgrading a plan or substituting guessed spreads is not authorized.

## Report and live boundary

Publish only accepted, prospectively collected ledger rows, with as-of dates, number of sessions/periods, both cost cases, holdings, trades, source hashes, errors and execution limitations. Waiting has null performance, no holdings or simulated fills, and no synthetic curve. Historical H3 results are in a separately labelled section. Missing or stale delivery is an operational error, not a financial zero-return pass. Original Core, earlier research and Buy Now remain separate.

The automatic report may say COLLECTING, DATA_BLOCKED, FORWARD_NO_GO or FORWARD_PASS_REVIEW_REQUIRED. The last status means the fixed financial sample passes, while independent execution-feasibility review is still required. The first-30-period financial verdict is preserved separately and never selected again using a later endpoint. The report NEVER sets live_approved or broker_orders_enabled. Real-money trading still requires explicit user approval. The exact policy is stored in `h3_forward_policy.json`; future administrative changes cannot rewrite the frozen strategy or the initial validation verdict.

Independent rejection quarantines the new attempt and restores the previous accepted prefix before durable publication.
