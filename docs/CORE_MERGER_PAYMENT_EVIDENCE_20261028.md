OCC memoranda 54106 (IMGN), 54268 (AMAM), 54611 (ALPN), 54670
(FUSN), and 58126 (CDTX) confirm merger consideration and option contract
adjustments. Their option settlement provisions do not certify when a stock
holder's broker credited merger proceeds; they are not used as stock payment
facts.

The Massive endpoint catalog search identified dividend pay-date data and
filing/event endpoints, but did not identify a stock-merger brokerage credit
date endpoint. No new subscription or endpoint purchase was made.

This review has NOT proven that the proceeds actually remained unpaid. The
indefinite lock is a deliberately conservative availability policy in the
original run; it is not a historical fact and need not be conservative for
every performance statistic. Cash timing sensitivity tests are separately
labelled hypothetical and do not fill the eight missing facts.

To validate actual reinvestment timing, dated paying-agent/DTC distribution
records or broker corporate-action credit records are still required. No
messages to third parties were sent during this review.
